## 👨‍⚕️ About Me

I am a **Healthcare IT professional** with **2+ years of hands-on experience** in healthcare interoperability and a **Master’s degree in Healthcare Data Analytics**. My work sits at the intersection of **clinical data, healthcare standards, and applied data science**.

I have practical experience designing and implementing **HL7 v2, FHIR, and X12 integrations** using integration engines such as **Iguana**, along with **Lua scripting, SQL, and API-based workflows**. Alongside interoperability, I am actively transitioning into **Healthcare Data Science**, focusing on **ML/DL models for clinical risk prediction, medical imaging, and responsible AI**.

My long-term objective is to build **end-to-end healthcare AI systems** that are:
- Clinically meaningful  
- Standards-compliant (FHIR-first)  
- Ethically designed and explainable  

I am particularly interested in **AI for population health, radiology, and decision support systems**, and I am preparing research-oriented projects aligned with **PhD and advanced research roles** in healthcare AI.
